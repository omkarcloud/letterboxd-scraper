Avatar: Fire and Ash

2025
Watched 29 Dec 2025 7

Lovely to be a part of this world again and spend some time (quite a lot) with these characters we know. Some of them are being fleshed out a bit more, which is nice. 

I just wish they would put as much effort in the story as they do in the looks. This is just Way of Water 2.0, amped up the spectacle and added a few new characters. And it annoys me that almost all humans are still caricatures.…

Eternals

2021
Rewatched 23 Feb 2025 14

I’ve always liked this better than the average viewer and I also think it holds up well on rewatches. It’s nowhere near perfect of course, but I love how it brings some sort of cosmic mystery to the MCU. It’s just such a shame how terribly bland Madden and Chan are as the leads. Especially with an excellent supporting cast with much more interesting characters. Let’s hope we get to see them fleshed out some more in the future.
